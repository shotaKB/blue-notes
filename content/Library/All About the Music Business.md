*音楽ビジネス* [All About the Music Business] <br>
Suzuki Takayuki

This book came from my company's library (currently has two books!), which makes sense considering we are definitely involved in the music business. It's a nice introductory to the contemporary music scene including streaming, social media marketing, the rise of DIY artists, and the use of AI. The book is made up of very concise chapters and I was already familiar with most of the contents so it was a nice easy read for me.

One thing I found interesting was the author's distinction of an indie artist vs a DIY artist (he calls it "Artist Direct"). It's a way to further break down non-major artists into "Independent", or people who release music through indie record labels, and "DIY", people who do no rely on a traditional labels to distribute and market their music. I've heard some musicians call themselves a DIY artist and really didn't know what they meant exactly until I read this description. In Japan the vocaloid scene seem to produce artists who fit this definition as the majority of these people become famous online while managing the entire workload by themselves or with a small team

I contributed *You Have Not Yet Heard Your Favourite Song* to the library so hopefully more people bring in interesting music related books.
