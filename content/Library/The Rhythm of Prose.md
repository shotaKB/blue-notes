*いい音がする文章* [The Rhythm of Prose]<br>
Takahashi Kumiko

This essay is filled with rhythmical writing, both by the author and by other Japanese writers. We all know words have meanings, but the author presents that they also have their own sounds. I definitely agree that sentences have their own rhythm attached to them. I'm kind of a chill person by nature and that gets reinforced when I speak in English, probably because I'm from the West Coast. When I speak Japanese it comes out a little faster because I have a kansai accent.

The author, being a former lyricist for her own band, brings up some classic Japanese lyrics and explains how they flow with distinct 5 - 7 rhythm. This is the same rhythm that Japanese poetry like the haiku and tanka are based on. I don't go as far as aligning the number of characters to fit that template, but I do feel like Japanese singing have a very distinct pace to it because of the strong vowel sounds. The later chapters are a gold mine for lyric writing, with the author sharing how she approaches this art form as well as going into actual techniques.
