*Naked Statistics* <br>
Charles Wheelan

An awesome intro to statistics. The book really focuses on explaining the concepts without going into the complex math, so it's great for people who want an introduction to stats. I was looking to grasp the basic concepts I can apply to my actual work in data collection and visualization, so the book worked really well for me too. Those busses breaking down in successive chapters is hilarious, especially because statistically it must be very rare for even one bus to break down.

Read this, watch [StatQuest](https://www.youtube.com/c/joshstarmer) to fill in the gaps, may be work out some exercises on [Think Stats](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkStats/), now you don't need to nod uncertainly when homie brings up "standard deviation" and "normal distribution" over coffee.
