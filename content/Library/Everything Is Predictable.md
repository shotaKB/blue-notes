*Everything Is Predictable* <br>
Tom Chivers

A great introductory book to the Bayes' Theorem and Bayesian statistics! Much like *[[Naked Statistics]]*, this book talks about examples of Bayesian thinking that can be found in the real world without going too deep into the math. For some reason I thought this was an older book, so I was caught off guard when it started talking about AI, the Ukraine invasion, and other current events.

One of the benefits of getting introduced to a wide range of ideas and theories is that I get to have an expanded set of options to tackle a problem. When I need to make prediction models, for example, I can contemplate whether I should apply Bayesian thinking or not. Can't do that if I don't know about Thomas Bayes to begin with. I'm hoping to build further on this introductory reading as I go deeper in the field of machine learning.
