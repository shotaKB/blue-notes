*WTF is Tarot? & How Do I Do It?* <br>
Bakara Winter

I told a coworker that I can read tarot. Within two weeks I was giving a reading to half of my office. So I figured the only responsible thing to do here is to buy a couple of books on this subject and get to learning. The great thing about this book is that it doesn't read like a boring textbook. The author puts in personal experiences about many of the cards instead of focusing on the universal meaning. However this can be a little bit of a double edged sword. I felt like some of the sections, like explaining the court cards as a family, were too personified to the point I really couldn't connect with it. I think of them more as personality traits rather than actual people, so using a strict family setting for these just confused me more.

Having said that, it's always interesting to view these cards from someone else's perspective. I probably didn't agree with about half of them, but it's still entertaining and educational to see just how differently we can interpret a certain card. The artwork was awesome too. A lot of very cute drawings!
