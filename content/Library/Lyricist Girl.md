*Lyricist Girl* <br>
Ogi Hinata

This is a very polarizing book! The actual contents are immensely helpful for writing lyrics. It gets into both the practical side, like helpful techniques when starting out the writing process, as well as the more fundamental themes touching on the human mind, history, and values. One thing I tried and got immediate results was singing the melody only using vowels and then using that as the jumping off point to fit the actual words in. It's an efficient way to find the sound that goes well with each note, especially in a language like Japanese where the sound is choppy and distinct.

The actual story is mediocre at best. The plot and character development is about as shallow as you can get, the conflict is predictable, and the resolution is forgettable. I almost never read light novels so maybe this is typical for this genre. To me its more like a textbook to study the art of writing lyrics. And as a textbook it's definitely one of the best out there. Definitely will recommend to anyone who makes music and wants to write better words for it.
