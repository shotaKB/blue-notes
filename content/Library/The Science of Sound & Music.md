*音と音楽の科学* [The Science of Sound & Music] <br>
Iwamiya Shinichiro

This book seems like it covers the entire audio world, starting from the physical explanation of sound, how instruments work, and ending with sound design. The first few chapters were very interesting to me as it talked about how sound travels through different mediums, what waves are, and the different harmonics you can find in the sounds around you. It goes into physics equations though, so I can see why people will just tune it out if they aren't much of a math person.

The later chapters talk about more relatable topics, like how different instruments make a sound. The illustrations on their did a great job of explaining these concepts. It briefly touched on electronic music production too, and how a lot of folks are now able to make music in their bedrooms with the help of modern software. Overall the book is the definition of "skimming the surface". It's a great primer for people who really have no idea how sound works in real life, but you are going to need to dig deeper on your own if you find anything that interest you in the book.
