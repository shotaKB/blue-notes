*From Words to Sound: Neural Audio Synthesis of Guitar Sounds with Timbral Descriptors*<br>
**Authors**: The Sound of AI Community<br>
**Year:** 2022<br>
[Article Link](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/370024900_From_Words_to_Sound_Neural_Audio_Synthesis_of_Guitar_Sounds_with_Timbral_Descriptors_The_Sound_of_AI_Community)

Making a voice command guitar synth sounds like an ambitious project. The interesting thing about this synth is that the team focused on timbral vocabulary grouped in three categories using the "Luminance-Texture-Mass model”. Some descriptors include “dark”, “clear”, “pure”, and “noisy”. It must be possible to reverse this process and analyze music and score them with these timbral descriptors as well. Also of note is that the research group collectively decided on these words, so there were some manual “tagging” happening as well.
