## Upright
- This card feels like a relief, a nice bench to rest on right after going up a huge hill. You haven't recovered from the climb, nor have you considered what can be done better to make the next climb easier. You just need the time to rest your mind and body.
- The "journey" aspect of the Ace - 10 sequence feels the most prevalent for the wands since they are about experiences and adventures. The six is a reminder that you've survived the hardships but are still firmly in the middle of your journey.
