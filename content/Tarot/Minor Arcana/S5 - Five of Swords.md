## Upright
- Five is the conflict card and the design looks like a brave warrior to me. I take it as tackling the problem head first, even at some cost, to see where that takes you. Then you can move on to the six through nine where you reflect on the conflict, think about what worked and what didn't work as well, and gather experience for your next battle.
