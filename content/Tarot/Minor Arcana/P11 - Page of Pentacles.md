## Upright
- This is a perfect fit for the "mind" card, someone who is very eager but lacks experience. Together with the [[P1 - Ace of Pentacles]], I saw a person who worries about how they come off towards others and constantly second guessing their decisions.
- On my Fate/Flowers deck the four Aces are designed like master mages. It's a beautiful contrast against the pages who are like novices or apprentices.
