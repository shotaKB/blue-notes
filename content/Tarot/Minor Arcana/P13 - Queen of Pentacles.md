## Upright
- This queen possess a pride in her wealth. It's a very "physical" type of inner power, opposite from [[C13 - Queen of Cups]].
- That's a big piece of coin she's holding. She has to rest it on her knees, must be one heavy object. Wealth carries weight, both physically and metaphorically.
