## Upright
- Since wands are about adventures and career progressions, this is the embodiment of a leader or someone who has gone through numerous journeys.
- This king feels softer and rounder than [[S14 - King of Swords]]. They are both leaders, but the swords lean more into battles and logic. This king is about the experiences and the knowledge we picked up along the way.
- If you see yourself as one of the kings, and think it's holding you back, can you imagine yourself in a different king? It might be too much work to try to change the court and the suit, so try changing into another king.
