## Reversed
- My basic idea for the queens and the kings is internal vs external. For the wands, it's the planning and the preparation of the mind before you venture into a new journey.
- We often sacrifice the time to sit down with ourselves and have monologues to clarify things that worry or trouble us. To me, this card signifies that internal conversation that we sometimes need to figure out where we are at.
- The self reflection doesn't always have to stay in your mind. Writing down in a journal, or on Obsidian is a great way to organize your thoughts and see it in front of you. Art is one of the strongest forms of self expression. I can say things through music that I just don't have the power to with just words. Or maybe you need to go get some brushes and start oil painting.
