- I see this as the most aggressive of the four knight cards. The face is ready for battle and the sword is drawn.
- Taking action could be a good thing at times, but this card feels very tough regardless. It's either you go through with your plans and completely butcher it, or you get the job done but don't feel much satisfaction.
- Knights take oath to the royalty. When you are hopelessly pushed ahead by your inner knight, it might be time to consult [[III - The Empress]] and just chill for a while.
