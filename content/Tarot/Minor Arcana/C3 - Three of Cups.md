## Upright
- Literally this is a party, a gathering, a meeting. Figuratively this could be the desire to get along with others, or to blend yourself in to the scene.
- Seeing two close numbers together pronounces new meanings, such as this card and either the [[C2 - Two of Cups]] or [[C4 - Four of Cups]]. Are they evolving, or pronouncing similarities? Can they be interchangeable? Can you try to flip one for the other?
