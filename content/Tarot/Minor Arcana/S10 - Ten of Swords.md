## Reversed
- I find all of the tens to be leading back to the ones. Since the swords are about logical thinking, the ten could be the act of thinking something through to the end. Not just wondering but constructing the logic as you go through your thoughts. Once you can do that, you might be able to start noticing how the end now connects to the new beginning, i.e. how one decision leads to the start of a new question.
