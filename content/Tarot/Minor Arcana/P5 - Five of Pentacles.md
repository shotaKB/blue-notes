- The imagery on this card is very clear to me. A lost person with no shelter wondering about the wilderness. It could simply be a material hardship, such as poverty or losing something important. On the other hand, the more mythical look would be that the person is lost or confused and is detaching from the material world.
- Sometimes the most romantic gesture is a silent hug. Be the earth, the grounding presence, and be there for each other during a time of hardship or confusion.
- This could be an antithesis to the [[S11 - Page of Swords]] when it comes to words and actions. When you are surrounded with irrational words or feel the need to get defensive with words, remember to let your actions speak. Try not to get blown down by the wind, and trust that your partner will do the same.
