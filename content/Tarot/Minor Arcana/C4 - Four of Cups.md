## Upright
- Four is the most stable number. It's when your relationship with someone is steady and comforting. You've gone past the initial surprise stage and know about that person deeper and feel comfortable around them.

## Reversed
- The character looks bored on this card. The opposite of stableness can be read as the unwillingness to try new things, staying in a safe zone, or laziness.
