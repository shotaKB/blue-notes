## Upright
- I think of the wands as your adventures, journeys, and experiences. For a career or job related reading, this card could literally be pointing at the querent's career path.
- Seeing this card as the "your path" card feels too fitting. I take it as almost a stable path that you have come so far, while the future is branching out into many different roads.
