- I think of the wands as your adventures, journeys, and experiences. For a career or job related reading, this card could literally be pointing at the querent's career path.
- Seeing this card as the "your path" card feels too fitting. I take it as almost a stable path that you have come so far, while the future is branching out into many different roads.
- Being the first card of the suit, it could be the journey itself, or signify the very beginning of a long road ahead. It may be the literal first step of your new journey.
- The Page and Ace share similar traits. [[S11 - Page of Swords]] is the protagonist holding a sword in her hand, ready to jump into the action. Then the Ace of Wands is the actual path that she is stepping foot in.
