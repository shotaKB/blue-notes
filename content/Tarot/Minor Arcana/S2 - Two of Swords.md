- This is the most obvious "choice" card, with the character holding a sword in both hands and presenting you with two options. It could be a choice you are going to encounter in the future, or maybe the idea of having options
- It's a darker card set at night, which makes the atmosphere more serious. It feels like a complex life choice rather than a simple yes/no.
- With this card reversed and [[S12 - Knight of Swords]] upright, I see the knight sword extending and intercepting the two swords. By following your passion and acting, or by [[III - The Empress|taking a break]] and scanning your options, you might break through into new choices that were not clear before.
