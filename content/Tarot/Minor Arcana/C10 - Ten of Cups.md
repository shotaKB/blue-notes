## Reversed
- I took this to mean the new beginning, but on a smaller scale, as it lay next to [[XXI - The World]].
- This card and [[C2 - Two of Cups]] both reversed could mean an unresolvable conflict unless you sit down and have a real conversation with the other person.
- The rainbow is a common theme across the cups. In my deck I see it in the [[C2 - Two of Cups|Two]], [[C6 - Six of Cups|Six]], [[C7 - Seven of Cups|Seven]], and Ten. I associate rainbows with peace and serenity, especially in the emotion and soul context.
- Since the cups are water, I can think of them as something that cools and refreshes your heart. If this card starts surrounding the other cards in the spread, you might be longing for comfort.
