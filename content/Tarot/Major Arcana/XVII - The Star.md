title: XVII - The Star
date: <% tp.date.now("YYYY-MM-DD") %>
---
- A star is both light and dark. Something that is bright and appealing, but not necessarily something you can attain. To me this card is the embodiment of the ideal. It's okay to look up to them and work hard to try to reach them while not getting all of the results.
- The Star holds two cups in each of his hands, so this card is related closely to the cup cards. The two cups are flowing the exact same amount of water into the river, resembling a perfect balance. The goal could be to try to achieve this balance if you are caught between two seemingly opposite ideas or feelings.
