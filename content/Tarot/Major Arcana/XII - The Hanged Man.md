- Similar to the Eight of Swords, this card is the pause and suspension before your awakening. You may feel trapped now, but often times that is the first step toward freedom
- Freeing yourself from the ropes may require letting go of old things, or heavy loads that you do not need to carry around anymore
- A voluntary surrender from the need to act or force and outcome. It's finding the inner peace by telling yourself "I'm just going to chill here and watch the sky because it is beautiful" and you don't worry about what other people have to say about yourself, because that's something outside your control.
- This card is similar to [[X - The Wheel of Fortune]] in that they both deal with ego. However The Wheel is about the outside chaos and tying your values to internal things so that the shallow temporal stuff (money, status, position) doesn't fling you around. The Hanged Man is about the conscious decision to finding peace within yourself and being okay with who you are.
