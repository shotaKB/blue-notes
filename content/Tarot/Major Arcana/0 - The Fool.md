- Possibly one of the best cards to see in the "action/advice" slot? The opportunity is there, but you need to be able to trust yourself, and the people around you, to take the leap of faith and find magic on the other side.
- It's important to understand that this is the beginning. Taking the first step may take a lot of courage, but there will be more hardships on the road ahead. Sometimes you fall flat on your face, and you have to be willing to get up and do it all over again.
- If you've successfully taken a big jump, then you know how much courage that takes. When someone else does it, make sure to acknowledge it, give them a good job!
- Not taking the jump may be the safer option. Not opening the door keeps you safe, but also can lead to lost opportunities.
