## Upright
- The basic concept of The Tower is the total destruction that cannot be avoided. Natural disasters are a good example.
## Reversed
- I had a fantastical reading where The Tower was sandwiched by two court cards. I took the knight and the queen as the guardians of the querent's path, and the mindset illustrated by those two as the advice that will protect the person from the sudden chaos.
