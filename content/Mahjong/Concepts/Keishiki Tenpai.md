## Overview
Getting to tenpai at the end of the round is an important part of mahjong, as you can gain points even if you don't win your hand. Say you were noten and another person had tenpai at the end of the round. That is a 1000 point payment. Now imagine you were the one keeping tenpai and getting 1000 points from each of the other players. That's a 4000 point difference!

## When to go for Keiten
If you have less than 3 turns left, it's a good time to start acting for keiten. Mathematically it is very hard to be at 1 shanten, draw a tile to get to tenpai, then draw another tile to win your hand all within 3 turns.
