## Overview
When you are trying to [[Folding|fold]] and don't have any genbutsu, look for tiles that have less chance of dealing in. One such tiles are the one chance tiles. In the below example, I can see three of the ![[Sou6.svg|20]] in the discards. The dealer must possess the last one of the ![[Sou6.svg|20]] in order to be waiting on a ![[Sou5.svg|20]]-![[Sou8.svg|20]], hence the name one chance. It's certainly not the safest of tiles, but much better than other options.

Also note that the ![[Man7.svg|20]] is [[Kabe|no chance]] here, although it being a dora will make a shanpon or penchan wait more probable.
