The goal of mahjong in the vast majority of the cases is to win your hand. You get rewarded with points even if you just end up in tenpai at draw. This means that you can push more tiles against a very early riichi compared to riichis in mid to late round. If you only have two more turns left, the reward for pushing diminishes significantly and folding usually becomes the better option.
