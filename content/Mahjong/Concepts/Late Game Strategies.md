## Overview
As you approach the South 3/4 rounds the stakes get higher and you might have to go for choices that may not make sense in earlier rounds. If you are winning, the goal is to obstruct the dealer and keep the rounds flowing. If you are in last place then being conscious of the point difference between you and third place is crucial.

## Avoiding 4th

![[late_game_strategies_01.png]]

This is a tight game where first and fourth are both very much possible. Let's go through the potential routes and outcomes. If I win this hand I either end up first or second but my hand is still at 1 shanten. Dealing in to either shimo or toimen will put me in fourth. The dealer has a guaranteed [[Simple Scoring Chard|mangan]] or better with the three visible doras, and toimen's hand must be at least 4000 points to overtake third place. So if I push I will sometimes get first but will likely end up fourth. If I fold and avoid dealing in then I will likely stay in second and will fall to third only on the rare occasion that kami wins the round. Therefore cutting a safe tile will maximize my point efficiency for this game.

## Unorganized Notes
Some notes on specific tactics
- Check if the score differential is greater than or less than 10000. [[Simple Scoring Chart|Mangan]] tsumo is worth 10000 points (8000 for the winner + 2000 lost from each opponent) so there is a chance of going up or down a position as long as the opponent is within this number. The calculation changes slightly if a dealer is involved. Don't worry about haneman and above as those are pretty rare.
