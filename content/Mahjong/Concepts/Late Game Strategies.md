As you approach the South 3/4 rounds the stakes get higher and you might have to go for choices that may not make sense in earlier rounds. If you are winning, the goal is to obstruct the dealer and keep the rounds flowing. If you are in last place then being conscious of the point difference between you and third place is crucial.

Some notes on specific tactics
- Check if the score differential is greater than or less than 10000. [[Simple Scoring Chart|Mangan]] tsumo is worth 10000 points (8000 for the winner + 2000 lost from each opponent) so there is a chance of going up or down a position as long as the opponent is within this number. The calculation changes slightly if a dealer is involved. Don't worry about haneman and above as those are pretty rare.
- Go for a quick 1000 or 2000 hand if you are in first, as this will keep the dealer from winning and moves the round forward. Not riiching could be a viable play as going dama will likely get your win rate up.
