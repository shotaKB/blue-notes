## Overview
When four of the same tiles are visible, then any tiles outside of it will be safe against ryanmen waits. This is called kabe (literally "a wall") or no chance and is useful when you are trying to [[Folding|fold]]
without any genbutsu.

In the below example, ![[Pin6.svg|20]] is four visible from my perspective. There is mathematically "no chance" of the other players holding a fifth ![[Pin6.svg|20]] to make ![[Pin8.svg|20]] the winning tile. Note that this only applies to ryanmen waits, so you cannot rule out other waits such as kanchan or shanpon unless you have other clues.
