## Overview
Five block theory is the "standard" method of hand building in modern mahjong. The goal is to maximize your wait at 1 shanten because going from 1 shanten to tenpai is what takes the most time.

Lets look at an example.

![[five_block_theory.png]]

If we break this hand up into blocks, we have ![[Man2.svg|20]]![[Man3.svg|20]]![[Man4.svg|20]] + ![[Man6.svg|20]]![[Man7.svg|20]]![[Man7.svg|20]] + ![[Pin6.svg|20]]![[Pin7.svg|20]] + ![[Sou1.svg|20]]![[Sou2.svg|20]] + ![[Sou7.svg|20]]![[Sou9.svg|20]] + ![[Pei.svg|20]]![[Pei.svg|20]]. That's six blocks, but we only need four complete sets and a head to win. Under five block theory, we drop the weakest block at this point instead of trying to keep all six.
